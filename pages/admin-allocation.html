Strict Min-Segregation Invariant
An order of size N oz with standard bar size B requires at most 1 partial bar (Fraction = N mod B) and floor(N / B) whole bars.

1. Whole Bar Greedy Match

The engine computes floor(Order / BarSize) and allocates whole 10 oz or 1 kg Good Delivery bars directly to the buyer's custodial account with 100% indivisible title.

Result: Zero fractional overhead for integer quotients.
02

2. Active Partial Bar Co-Location

For the remainder Order mod BarSize, the algorithm looks up the currently open partial bar in the nearest depository with available capacity, packing shares compactly.

Result: Never opens a new bar if existing bar has space.
03

3. Physical Redemption Consolidation

When an institution redeems physical gold, the consolidation worker merges any partial tokens with the treasury buffer, guaranteeing physical bar delivery in pristine whole units.

Result: 100% compliant with LBMA Physical Delivery Rules.
